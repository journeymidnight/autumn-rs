F173 — Persist ec_conversion_inflight to etcd:
  Why:
    F138's eversion-bump-lock semantics ("while X ∈
    ec_conversion_inflight, no other mutator bumps eversion") only
    hold within a single leader's lifetime — the HashSet was purely
    in-memory. On leader failover the new leader's set was empty,
    and recovery_dispatch_loop could fire re_avali /
    require_recovery on extents the deposed leader was actively
    converting. F119-D + F153 + F119-C made the race non-corrupting
    in practice, but each fires post-hoc — F173 closes the window
    at the source.

  What:
    persist_ec_conversion_inflight(extent_id):
      etcd PUT ecConversionInflight/{id} (empty value, existence
      is the signal). Called BEFORE EXT_MSG_CONVERT_TO_EC dispatch
      in ec_conversion_dispatch_loop. On etcd failure, skip this
      extent and retry next tick.

    unpersist_ec_conversion_inflight(extent_id):
      etcd DELETE. Called AFTER apply_ec_conversion_done or RPC
      failure. Lingering markers are harmless (one extra
      recovery_dispatch_loop skip + idempotent reconvert).

    replay_from_etcd:
      scan `ecConversionInflight/` prefix, repopulate
      ec_conversion_inflight: HashSet<u64> BEFORE the leader-key
      flip completes. New leader's first recovery_dispatch_loop
      tick observes markers and skips those extents.

  Why both F173 (etcd persist) and F153 (extent-node lock):
    - F153 catches CONCURRENT dispatch race: deposed leader's
      encode mid-flight when new leader re-fires. Per-extent
      futures::lock::Mutex serialises; second dispatch waits,
      F119-D makes it a no-op.
    - F173 catches BROADER recovery race: deposed leader's EC
      dispatch may have completed on coordinator (shards on disk,
      eversion bumped) but apply_ec_conversion_done never ran in
      etcd. recovery_dispatch_loop would dispatch require_recovery
      on the eversion mismatch; F173's marker tells new leader to
      skip until the next ec_conversion_dispatch_loop re-enters
      and converges state.
