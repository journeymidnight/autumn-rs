Root cause (confirmed):
  F138 made ec_conversion_inflight an eversion-bump lock but missed two
  mutators. Both handle_stream_punch_holes and handle_truncate fell into

  silently rewrote replica assignments on a stale record. On leader-

  after the F139 recovery guards in each handler. The guards scan
  `removed` and refuse the entire RPC if any extent is in
