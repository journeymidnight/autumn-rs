Audit produced 12 checks; 4 issues found, rest walked clear:

  HIGH-1: futures::channel::mpsc capacity is buffer + num_senders, not
          buffer. compact_tx/gc_tx with cap=1 + 2 senders = 3 backlog.
          Scheduler + manual triggers could each enqueue without
          drain. Fixed by draining at receive time and collapsing
          (compact: any major wins; gc: union Force extents, drop
          redundant Autos).
  HIGH-2: last_gc_at / last_compact_at only stamped on success; the
          scheduler's cooldown gate read 0-as-never and re-dispatched
          every 5 s under no-op (empty holes) or transient extent-node
          failure. Fixed by stamping on every loop iteration that ran
          the eligibility check (semantic shift: "last evaluation"
          rather than "last successful punch", which is what cooldown
          actually wants).
  MED-3:  scheduler's req_per_sec diff against req_count raced
          report_load_loop's swap-reset; FG-quota gate read 0 during
          real FG storms. Fixed by adding never-reset
          PartitionMetrics.req_count_monotonic; scheduler diffs
          against this. Two atomic adds per request (~5 ns).
  MED-4:  gc_inflight / compact_inflight latched after gate.acquire(),
          which can block for seconds. During that window scheduler
          fired redundant dispatches. Fixed by latching at top of
          receive arm + explicit clear at every exit path (inline
          closures keep the call sites tight).
