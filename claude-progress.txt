=== HOW F254 + F255 LAYER ON 95efeba ===

  95efeba gates the SOURCE: no new compact starts during freeze, drain
  waits for compact_inflight==0 before ACK. That closes the in-flight
  race window.

  F255 gates the SINK + adds explicit cache invalidation:
    - per-partition `compact_gate: Arc<CompactionGate>(max=1)` —
      mutual exclusion split↔compact on THIS partition even OUTSIDE
      the freeze window. The PS-wide `ConcurrencyController.
      acquire_compact` permit (default max=4) does NOT serialize
      same-partition; compact_gate does. Belt-and-braces with
      95efeba's frozen-check.
    - synchronous `RowInvalidateBarrierReq` sent by handle_split_part
      BETWEEN commit_length capture and multi_modify_split. P-bulk
      drains its FuturesUnordered to zero (handles any rare residual
      ops the frozen-check might miss across future refactors) and
      calls `bulk_sc.invalidate_stream(row_stream_id)` so the cached
      per-stream worker is discarded — fixes the post-seal cache-
      staleness gap that 95efeba doesn't address (without the
      explicit invalidate, the next P-bulk op after freeze release
      would use bulk_sc's pre-seal cached tail).
    - Bounded send (5s) + ACK (10s) timeouts on the barrier; total 15s
      well under FREEZE_TTL (30s) so TTL race can't extend the window
      and let stale commit_length reach multi_modify_split.

  F254 is orthogonal: removes the in-thread fallback path in
  run_flush_async_phase + compact_row_append, so row_stream's single-
  writer invariant is type-level (PartitionData.{flush_req_tx,
  row_append_tx} are non-Option<Sender>). Was the structural foothold
  for 2026-05-03 invalid_meta_len: two StreamClients each tracked
  commit watermark on the same row_stream, one's stale commit
  truncated the other's SST bytes per commit-protocol step 5.
