Design note (regpool / UCX auto bcopy-vs-zcopy — user asked, decided to KEEP):
  UCX auto-selects bcopy/zcopy and even auto-registers via rcache, BUT only
  amortizes if buffers are REUSED — UCX can't decide to reuse YOUR buffers, and
  the recv must land in a specific dest. So regpool's real job = buffer reuse +
  cancel-safe lifecycle, NOT protocol switching (sends already fully UCX-managed,
  no regpool). Decision: keep the proven memh+regpool; the simplification (drop
  explicit memh, lean on rcache + reuse; production = register the sglang pool
  once so the standalone regpool isn't needed on the real path) is a follow-up.
