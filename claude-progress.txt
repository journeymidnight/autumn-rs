Atomicity headline:
  Pre-F207-B `apply_ec_conversion_done` did:
    1. etcd put_msgs_txn(extents/<id>) — one network round-trip
    2. in-memory remove from ec_conversion_inflight + pending_ec_dispatch
    3. etcd put_and_delete_txn(deletes=[ecConversionInflight/<id>]) —
       another round-trip
  Manager crash between steps 1 and 3 leaked the etcd marker. New
  leader's replay reloaded it, blocking future EC convert dispatch
  on that extent indefinitely (the F119-D coordinator-side guard
  made it a stall, not corruption — but still a bug).

  Post-F207-B:
    1. mutate in-memory state
    2. ONE etcd put_and_delete_txn(
         puts=[extents/<id>],
         deletes=[extent_inflight/<id>, ecConversionInflight/<id>]
       ) — single txn under F149 leader fence
    3. in-memory commit_extent_inflight_release (after etcd OK,
       per etcd-first invariant)
  Either both etcd keys move together or neither does. Marker
  leaks impossible. Invariant I3 enforced structurally.

  Pattern: where the value was consulted multiple times inside a
  closure under a borrow_mut of the store, a snapshot
  `ec_inflight_set: HashSet<u64>` is taken at the top of the
  handler. Single-threaded compio + snapshot-then-consult
  preserves the semantics of the old `let ec_inflight =
  self.ec_conversion_inflight.borrow();` pattern.
