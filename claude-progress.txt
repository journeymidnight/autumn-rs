A burst of >= 1 MiB is written "tail-block rewrite": [a0,start) read back from the page cache and copied in front of the payload into a 4 KiB-aligned bounce buffer (over-allocated Vec, no unsafe, 8 MiB pieces, 4 pooled per shard), [a0,floor(end)) O_DIRECT through a per-burst reopen of the resident fd via /proc/self/fd, then the sub-block tail buffered, then the burst's usual single sync_data. Both append paths (owner append_burst_frames, handle_append) go through write_append_burst. At startup (flag on only) each data dir's disk_id is opened read-only with O_DIRECT; EINVAL (no O_DIRECT, e.g. tmpfs < 6.6) stops the node. Nothing is written, so a full/failing disk cannot be confused with missing O_DIRECT (an earlier write+read-back probe was replaced after three reviews kept finding disk-state ambiguities; user chose the open-only check). The file is never padded (its size is the extent length after restart, which the commit protocol trusts).
