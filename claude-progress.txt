=== REPRODUCTION (heavy workload + concurrent compacts + splits) ===

  ./cluster.sh reset 4
  ./target/release/autumn-client --manager 127.0.0.1:9001 \
      perf-check --threads 128 --duration 60 --size 4096 &
  sleep 3
  for i in 1..5; do
      autumn-op compact 15 &
      sleep 1
      autumn-op split 15 &
      sleep 2
  done
  wait
  ./cluster.sh stop; ./cluster.sh start 4

  Pre-fix observed:
    ERROR partition thread failed: open SST extent=22 offset=0
      read_len=267968812 ...: invalid meta_len=269422093 part_id=29

  Same failure CLASS as the original `stale_vp_offset_past_sealed_length`:
  TableLocations record references a row_stream offset that points
  past where the manager sealed the extent. Whether the read fires
  `stale_vp_offset` (read offset > sealed_length) or `invalid meta_len`
  (read offset within sealed_length but the requested LENGTH spans past
  it) depends on which TableLocations entry the recovery scan hits
  first. Both are the same upstream cause.

=== ROOT CAUSE (second race, not covered by e40ce48) ===

  Site: crates/partition-server/src/background.rs::do_compact
        crates/partition-server/src/lib.rs::try_complete_freeze_drain

  e40ce48 made the drain wait for `flushing_imm_ptrs` claims to clear
  (the FlushStep::Busy retry) — covering `background_flush_loop`'s
  in-flight Phase 2. But `do_compact` ALSO writes SSTs to row_stream
  via a SEPARATE path:

      do_compact (P-log task)
        → compact_row_append (sends RowAppendReq on row_append_tx)
        → P-bulk's flush_worker_loop
        → P-bulk's StreamClient.append on row_stream
        → EN-side pwrite + fsync, ACK back through the chain

  None of this is tracked in `flushing_imm_ptrs` — compact doesn't
  claim any imm. So `try_complete_freeze_drain`'s flush_one_imm loop
  finds imm empty + no claims and returns Empty IMMEDIATELY, signaling
  split_drain_ack.

  Concrete failure timeline:
    1. bg_compact_loop is mid-do_compact for partition X. It sent a
       compact_row_append and is awaiting the P-bulk response. EN's
       last_synced on row_stream's tail extent does NOT yet cover the
       in-flight bytes.
    2. Split RPC arrives. handle_split_part sets `frozen_for_split`,
       partition_loop runs try_complete_freeze_drain.
    3. Drain calls flush_one_imm — imm is empty (no flush in flight)
       → FlushStep::Empty → break. Signal split_drain_ack.
    4. handle_split_part captures commit_length(row_stream). All-replica
       last_synced is at the PRE-compact value.
    5. multi_modify_split fires. compute_duplicate_stream seals
       row_stream's tail at the stale length.
    6. compact's compact_row_append finally lands on all replicas.
       EN's last_synced advances past sealed_length.
    7. do_compact's atomic-swap step (`save_table_locs_raw`) publishes
       a TableLocations record referencing the new SST at the
       post-seal offset.
    8. On next PS open, recover_partition reads the TableLocations
       record, hits read_bytes_from_extent at (extent_id, offset_past_seal,
       len), surfaces `stale_vp_offset_past_sealed_length` OR
       `invalid meta_len` (offset within sealed_length but length
       spans past it; the latter is what the 2026-06-02 reproduction
       observed).

  Also affected: the F185 merge-freeze path. Same drain, same gap.

  Forensics summary (one failing extent in the reproduction):
    Manager etcd:   sealed=true, sealed_length=226 MB
    EN .meta (V2):  sealed=false, sealed_length=0, eversion=1 (EN
                    never received the seal)
    Physical file:  749 MB on all 3 replicas (full data)
    TableLocations row: extent=22 offset=0 read_len=267968812
                        (~256 MB, past sealed_length=226 MB)

=== FIX (shipped, 2026-06-02) ===

  Patch: crates/partition-server/src/lib.rs
         crates/partition-server/src/background.rs

  1. `try_complete_freeze_drain` (lib.rs, after the flush_one_imm
     loop): polls `part.metrics.compact_inflight` and sleeps
     `FLUSH_BUSY_RETRY_INTERVAL` (2 ms) until it reaches 0 before
     signaling split_drain_ack / freeze_drain_ack. Uses the existing
     PartitionMetrics.compact_inflight counter (F187, latched at the
     top of bg_compact_loop's recv arm and cleared on every exit
     branch). Only added on the drain_err.is_none() path so a flush
     failure still propagates promptly.

  2. `background_compact_loop` (background.rs, top of the recv arm
     after the channel drain): refuses to start a new compact when
     `frozen_for_split.is_some() || frozen_for_merge.is_some()`.
     Silent skip — the maintenance scheduler / manual `autumn-op
     compact` retries naturally after the freeze clears. Without
     this, the drain's compact_inflight=0 wait could ack the freeze,
     handle_split_part captures commit_length, and a FRESHLY-
     dispatched compact starts its row_stream append — re-introducing
     the race after the freeze.

  Together these establish the invariant: while frozen_for_split or
  frozen_for_merge is set on a partition, ZERO row_stream writes can
  be in flight or initiated on that partition. The drain only signals
  the freeze ack once that invariant holds. handle_split_part's
  commit_length capture is then race-free against compact, mirroring
  how e40ce48 made it race-free against flush.

  Build: `cargo build --release -p autumn-server` PASSES.
  Lib tests: 148/148 pass (`cargo test --release -p
             autumn-partition-server --lib`).
