Why this closes a real gap:
  Pre-F194 a recovery_dispatch_loop tick after a node-down event
  could detached-spawn N concurrent recoveries on the same survivor
  node (one per down extent). Each holds payload × 2 transient
  memory through fetch + writeback. 6 simultaneous recoveries on 3
  GiB extents → ~36 GiB peak. Manager's serial .await on dispatch
  RPC doesn't gate node-side execution since require_recovery
  early-returns and run_recovery_task is detached.

  F194's gate caps this at parallelism × single-task peak ≈ 12 GiB
  (recovery) or 7 GiB (EC, parallelism=1). Together with F193 Stage
  A (jemalloc page return), steady-state RSS returns to live-heap
  within ~1 s of the spike subsiding.
