Stale-marker sweep (new):
  - `extent_inflight_stale_sweep_loop` in extent_inflight.rs:
    * 5-minute tick (longer than other background loops; stale
      markers are slow-burn).
    * 24-hour stale threshold from `started_at`.
    * WARN log per stale marker with extent_id + op kind + age
      seconds + remediation instructions.
    * Auto-clearing INTENTIONALLY NOT done — a stuck marker
      usually signals a real bug worth surfacing.
    * Operator runs Python ops `--clear-stale-inflight extent <id>`
      after investigating with `etcdctl get extent_inflight/<id>`.
  - Spawned by `start_runtime_tasks` alongside the other loops
    (recovery_dispatch, recovery_collect, disk_status_update,
    ec_conversion_dispatch, ps_liveness_check, extent_delete,
    policy_tick).
